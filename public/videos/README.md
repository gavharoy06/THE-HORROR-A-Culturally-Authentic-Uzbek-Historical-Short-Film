# DAHSHAT — Video Assets Directory

Place your production cinematic video files for each act in this folder:

- Act 1: `scene_1.mp4` or `scene_1.webm`
- Act 2: `scene_2.mp4` or `scene_2.webm`
- Act 3: `scene_3.mp4` or `scene_3.webm`

The player in Cinema Mode automatically detects these video files at `/videos/scene_1.mp4`, `/videos/scene_2.mp4`, and `/videos/scene_3.mp4`.
If video files have not been uploaded yet, the player automatically provides the restored 35mm archival cinematic stream with synchronized Web Audio soundscapes and subtitles.
